## Fonts

The interface loads [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) and [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans) through Google Fonts. These font families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1; font binaries are not bundled in this repository. Their upstream directories include the applicable copyright notices and license text. The publication images contain rasterized text.
